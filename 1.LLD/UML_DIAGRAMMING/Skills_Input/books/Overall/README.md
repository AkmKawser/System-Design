# Overall UML & Case Study References Library

This directory houses comprehensive reference books and case-study manuals covering end-to-end UML modeling across structural, behavioral, and architectural perspectives.

## 📚 Curated Books in this Directory

### 1. `UML Diagramming: A Case Study Approach`
- **File**: `[BEST] UML Diagramming.pdf`
- **Author**: Dr. Suriya Sundaramoorthy
- **Publisher**: CRC Press / Taylor & Francis Group (1st Edition, 2022)
- **Volume**: 431 pages, 40 Chapters
- **Chapters Archive**: [`chapters/`](chapters/)
- **Vector Images Archive**: [`vector_images/`](vector_images/) (All 477 diagrams & figures in Scalable Vector Graphics `.svg` format)
- **Focus**: Unified Process (UP) phases, comprehensive UML diagram components, and 39 end-to-end industrial case studies covering:
  - Healthcare & Telemedicine (`Webmed`, `Aarogya Setu`, `National Health ID 2020`, `e-Med`, `Equihealth`)
  - Transportation & Travel (`E-Ticketing for Buses`, `Smart Traffic Management`, `Green Rides`, `E-Visa Processing`)
  - Retail & Commerce (`Inventory Management`, `Online Marketplace`, `Product Recommendation`, `Dropshipping`)
  - Identity & Governance (`DigiDocLocker`, `E-Province`, `E-Ration Shop`, `Crime Bureau`)
  - Enterprise, Media & EdTech (`BPO`, `Student Counselling`, `Placement Automation`, `Book Bank`, `Online Quiz`, `OTT Mini Reel`)
